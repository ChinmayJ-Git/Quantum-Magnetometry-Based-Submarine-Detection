# Quantum Magnetometry Based Submarine Detection
MATLAB simulation codes and figures developed during my Research Internship at the Centre for Applied Research in Electronics (CARE), IIT Delhi under the guidance of Prof. Neel Kanth Kundu, CARE, IITD.

This repository accompanies the report titled "Quantum Magnetometry for Submarine Detection: A Theoretical Investigation of Sensing, Detection, and Localization." It contains the MATLAB scripts for simulations together with all figures used throughout the research report.
